# Munim AI v2 – Hardened Security & Accounting Architecture Changelog

## Overview
Munim AI v2 introduces mission-critical security boundaries, deterministic validation layers, abuse prevention, and statutory compliance updates tailored for Indian Chartered Accountants, tax practitioners, and MSME business owners.

---

## 🔴 1. Security & Firestore Rules Hardening
- **User Ownership Isolation:** All Firestore query records in the `queries` collection now require a valid authenticated `userId` matching `request.auth.uid`.
- **Private Data Protection:** Prevented cross-user leakages of journal vouchers, financial statement drafts, and vendor/customer records.
- **Anonymous Authentication:** Integrated Firebase Anonymous Auth (`ensureFirebaseAuth`) ensuring zero-friction client onboarding while maintaining strict database security rules.
- **Deployment:** Enforced via updated `firestore.rules` and `firebase-blueprint.json`.

---

## 🔴 2. AI & API Abuse Protection
- **Sliding-Window Rate Limiting:** Enforced a burst limit of max 25 requests per minute per client IP / auth token.
- **Tiered Daily Quotas:**
  - **Free Tier:** 40 AI queries / day, 15 OCR scans / day
  - **Student Tier:** 100 AI queries / day, 30 OCR scans / day
  - **CA Professional Tier:** 500 AI queries / day, 150 OCR scans / day
- **Payload Controls:**
  - Maximum body size restricted.
  - Receipt scanner payloads capped at 10 MB base64.
  - Sanitization of message history and prompt character bounds.
- **HTTP 429 Status:** Clear structured error responses with quota consumption details and reset timestamps.

---

## 🔴 3. Deterministic Accounting Integrity Layer
- **No Blind Trust in LLM Arithmetic:** All journal vouchers generated by AI pass through `validateAndEnforceVoucher`.
- **Double-Entry Equilibrium:** Mathematically verifies that `Total Debit === Total Credit` (tolerance < ₹0.01).
- **GST Statutory Validation:** Recalculates CGST, SGST, and IGST based on place-of-supply (Intra-state vs Inter-state) and applies Section 16 eligibility.
- **TDS Validation:** Validates TDS deductions against statutory thresholds (e.g. ₹30,000 for 194J/194C, ₹2,40,000 for 194I, ₹15,000 for 194H, ₹50,000 for 194M, ₹50,00,000 for 194Q) under both Income-tax Act, 1961 and Income-tax Act, 2025 (Section 393).
- **Audit Badge Accuracy:** Replaced premature "CA Verified" labels with transparent "Arithmetic Validated • Review Facts" tags, reminding users that commercial facts require professional accountant scrutiny.

---

## 🔴 4. OCR & Vision Safety
- **No Fabricated Fallbacks:** Completely eliminated artificial fallbacks (e.g., fake ₹2,500 bills, dummy GSTINs, or invented vendor names).
- **Explicit Uncertainty Flags:** If a receipt has missing or unconfirmed dates/years, the system marks `yearUncertain: true` and requests manual date confirmation rather than guessing the current year.
- **Potential vs Verified ITC:** Clarified in compliance notes that input tax credits remain potential until matched with supplier filings in GSTR-2B under Section 16(2)(aa).

---

## 🟠 5. Trial Balance Importer & Ind AS Classification
- **Confidence Scoring:** Account classifications now return high, medium, or low confidence with audit reasons.
- **Review Triggers:** Accounts containing "Suspense", "Diff in TB", or unrecognized counterparty patterns are flagged for mandatory review (`reviewRequired: true`).
- **Asset Nature Safeguards:** Security deposits and electricity deposits are properly categorized under Non-Current Financial Assets rather than operating expenses.
- **Clear Review Flags:** Manual override by the user immediately clears the review flag.

---

## 🟠 6. Tax Calendar & Statutory Deadlines
- **Dynamic Real-Time Engine:** Replaced static date references with runtime system dates.
- **Tax Audit Report Due Date Clarification:**
  - **Statutory Due Date:** 30th September of the Assessment Year under Section 44AB read with Section 139(1).
  - **CBDT Extended Due Date:** 07th October 2026 for non-transfer pricing cases.
  - **Transfer Pricing Cases (Sec 92E):** 31st October 2026 (with ITR due on 30th November).
  - **Section 271B Penalty:** Clearly highlights 0.5% penalty up to ₹1,50,000 for delayed filing.
- **Official Government Portals:** Linked directly to `incometax.gov.in`, `eportal.incometax.gov.in`, `gst.gov.in`, and `tdscpc.gov.in`.
